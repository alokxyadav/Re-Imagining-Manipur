Accessible travel: what to ask before you book

This guide doesn’t hold verified accessibility details for individual places, so please confirm directly. Useful questions when you call a hotel or site:

  • Is there a step-free entrance, and a lift if rooms are above ground level?
  • Is a ground-floor room available, and how wide is the bathroom door?
  • Are there grab rails or a shower seat?
  • Can a taxi pull up to the door, and how far is the walk from the road?
  • For forts, lakeside and market visits: are the paths paved or uneven, and is seating available?

Getting there

Imphal Tulihal Airport is about 8 km from the city centre, with direct flights from Delhi, Kolkata, Guwahati and Hyderabad.

Imphal has no railway station of its own, the airport is the practical way in for most travellers.

Best time to visit

September to April brings clear skies and moderate temperatures, ideal for Kangla Fort and long afternoons at Loktak Lake.

Orchids at Khonghampat peak from April to July; the Sangai Festival runs in late November.

Entry permit

Indian citizens from outside Manipur need an Inner Line Permit, apply online at manipurilponline.mn.gov.in or on arrival at Imphal Airport.

Foreign nationals need a Protected Area Permit for certain zones, arrange this ahead of travel.

Good to know

Ima Keithel is run entirely by women traders and trades daily, go in the morning for the freshest produce and quieter aisles.

Modest dress is appreciated at Shree Govindajee Temple and other active places of worship.
