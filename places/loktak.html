Loktak Lake

Moirang, ~45 km south of Imphal · about 180 minutes

The largest freshwater lake in northeast India, famous for its floating phumdi islands, unlike anywhere else on Earth.

Loktak is ringed by floating phumdis, thick mats of vegetation and soil that host entire fishing villages on their surface. Sendra Island offers panoramic views from a hilltop, and boat rides thread between the floating islands. The lake is both an ecological marvel and a working landscape for local fishing communities.

Good to know

  • Take a boat from Sendra for the best views
  • Pair with the INA Memorial en route
  • Visit October–March for clearest water and skies
