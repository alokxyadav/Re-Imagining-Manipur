Kangla Fort

Imphal West, on the Imphal River · about 20 minutes

The original royal seat of the Manipuri kings, ringed by a moat and guarded by dragon-like Kangla-Sha statues.

Kangla was the seat of power for the Ningthouja dynasty until the British occupation, and remains the most important historical site in Imphal. Built with well-burned brick, its grounds hold the ruins of royal palaces, sacred ponds and the Shree Govindajee temple, blending ancient Meetei and colonial-era structures inside one walled archaeological park.

Good to know

  • Best visited in the cool morning hours
  • Combine with Shree Govindajee Temple next door
  • Guided walks explain the Kangla-Sha dragon symbolism
