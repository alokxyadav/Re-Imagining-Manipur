Andro Village

~25 km east of Imphal · about 90 minutes

A heritage craft village known for its pottery traditions and glimpses of pre-modern Meetei rural life.

Andro preserves traditions largely unchanged for generations, particularly hand-built pottery made without a wheel. A small heritage complex in the village walks visitors through Meetei rural life, rituals and craft.

Good to know

  • Best combined with a half-day countryside loop
  • Support local potters by buying directly
