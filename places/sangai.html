Sangai Festival Grounds

Multiple venues incl. Moirang & Imphal (late Nov) · about 180 minutes

Manipur’s flagship cultural festival, polo, boat races, Thang-Ta martial arts and craft stalls from across the state.

Named after the state’s emblematic brow-antlered deer, the Sangai Festival runs across late November, spreading events between Imphal and sites like the Sangai Ethnic Park near Moirang. Expect classical Ras-Leela performances, indigenous sport, food stalls and craft from every district.

Good to know

  • Plan travel dates around late November
  • Book accommodation well ahead, it’s the peak season
