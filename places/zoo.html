Manipur Zoological Garden

Iroisemba, ~8 km west of Imphal · about 90 minutes

The state’s main zoo and second home of the Sangai deer, set at the foot of the pine-covered Iroisemba hills.

Opened in 1976 at the foot of the Iroisemba hillocks, this is a designated conservation-breeding centre for the endangered Sangai (brow-antlered deer) and Hume’s pheasant, the state bird. It holds close to 400 species across birds, mammals and reptiles largely endemic to Manipur, making it a reliable place to see the Sangai even outside migratory season at Keibul Lamjao.

Good to know

  • Closed Mondays, plan around it
  • Entry is inexpensive; go in the cooler morning hours
  • A good family-friendly stop on the way to or from Khonghampat
