INA Martyrs’ Memorial Complex, Moirang

Moirang, Bishnupur district · about 60 minutes

Where the Indian National Army first hoisted the tricolour on Indian soil, on 14 April 1944, under Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose.

Established in 1985, the memorial and museum at Moirang display over a thousand objects and documents from the INA movement and the Second World War in Manipur. It marks one of the most significant moments of India’s freedom struggle to take place outside the mainland.

Good to know

  • Pair with Loktak Lake, Moirang sits right beside it
  • Museum closed on select government holidays
