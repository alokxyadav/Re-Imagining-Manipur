Khongjom War Memorial Complex

Khongjom, ~36 km from Imphal · about 40 minutes

Commemorates the 1891 Anglo-Manipuri War, where Major Paona Brajabashi led a defining last stand.

The Anglo-Manipur War of 1891 was the final resistance before Manipur became a British princely state. The memorial at Khongjom honours the soldiers who fought here, and the date is still marked annually as Khongjom Day across the state.

Good to know

  • A meaningful stop for those interested in 19th-century history
  • Combine with a Moirang/Loktak day trip
