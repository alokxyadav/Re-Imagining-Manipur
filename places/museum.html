Manipur State Museum

Imphal city centre · about 45 minutes

A compact but rich collection of Meetei manuscripts, royal artefacts, tribal costumes and archaeological finds.

The state museum houses palm-leaf manuscripts, royal regalia, ethnographic displays from Manipur’s many hill communities, and a natural-history wing. It’s a useful first stop for context before visiting Kangla Fort and the surrounding heritage sites.

Good to know

  • Good rainy-day option
  • Allow time for the tribal-costume gallery
  • Closed on some public holidays, check ahead
