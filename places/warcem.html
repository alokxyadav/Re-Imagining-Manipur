Imphal War Cemetery

Imphal city · about 30 minutes

A Commonwealth War Graves Commission site commemorating soldiers of the 1944 Battle of Imphal.

Immaculately maintained rows of stone markers and bronze plaques record the sacrifice of Allied soldiers who fought the Japanese army at Imphal in 1944, one of the turning-point battles of the Second World War in Asia. The cemetery is quiet and reflective, a short walk from the city centre.

Good to know

  • A calm, respectful visit, go early or late in the day
  • Combine with Kangla Fort on a half-day city loop
