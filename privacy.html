Privacy

Last updated 18 October 2026

This guide is an independent hackathon prototype. This page says plainly what it does with your information.

What stays on your device

Saved places, your trip, the packing checklist, place notes, reviews you write, and your theme and text-size choices are kept in your browser's local storage. They are not sent to the site owner. Clearing your site data removes them.

Email sign-in

When email sign-in is switched on, the address you enter is sent to our sign-in provider, Supabase, which emails you a one-time code and checks it. The guide then remembers on your device that you verified. In demo mode no email is sent and nothing leaves your device.

Newsletter box

In this prototype the box saves your address on your device only.

Third-party services

The page loads fonts from Google Fonts and a QR-code script from cdnjs, so those services can see your IP address and browser details when the page loads. There are no advertising or analytics trackers and no tracking cookies.

Your choices

You can clear your browser's site data at any time. To ask for a verified email address to be removed from the sign-in provider, write to [contact email].

Changes
